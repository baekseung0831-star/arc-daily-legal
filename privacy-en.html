LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Based on the current source-code review, ARC Daily stores goals, to-dos, journal entries, and other app records on your device and does not automatically upload those records to a developer server. Voluntary support emails and JSON backup sharing are explained separately below.

1. Operator and contact

This policy applies to the ARC Daily mobile app (the “App”) and its information website. ARC Daily is currently operated by an individual developer. For privacy inquiries, contact arcdaily.support@gmail.com.

2. Information processed and purposes

To provide its features, the App processes information you voluntarily enter, including a profile name and bio, routines, goals, to-dos, journal entries, progress history, badges, experience points, settings, and local notification preferences and records.

A source-code review of the current version found no user account system, developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cross-app and cross-website tracking features. We will update this policy if the final release configuration changes.

3. Storage and retention

Your entries are stored locally on your device using SQLite-based storage. Deleting the App ordinarily removes its local data, but JSON files you exported or copies in an iOS device backup may remain. Offloading an app is different from deleting it.

4. Notifications

With your permission, the App schedules local reminders on your device. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings. The current source code does not include a remote push notification delivery server or a push token upload mechanism.

5. Backup exports and sharing

When you choose “Export Backup”, the App prepares a JSON representation of your records and passes it to the iOS share sheet. It may contain sensitive journal or goal information. You decide which application or destination receives it, and that service may apply its own privacy policy. The current App does not provide an import or restore feature.

6. Support email

If you contact arcdaily.support@gmail.com, we may process your sending email address, message, and voluntary attachments to respond. We use Gmail to receive and manage support messages. Messages are kept as needed to resolve the request and for reasonable follow-up, subject to applicable retention obligations and deletion requests. Please avoid sending full backup files or sensitive personal data.

7. Website and other services

This information website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not install advertising or visitor analytics scripts in its source code. GitHub may process connection information under its own policies. Read the GitHub privacy information and Google Privacy Policy for more information.

8. Your rights and deletion

You may use the in-app record management tools and iOS permission settings. To remove all on-device App data, delete the App. The developer cannot remotely access or delete records stored only on your device. For access or deletion requests concerning your support emails, contact arcdaily.support@gmail.com. We respect rights provided by applicable privacy laws.

9. Children

ARC Daily is not specifically designed for children.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as the App, its storage and transfer practices, included SDKs, or applicable laws change. The effective date will appear on this page.
