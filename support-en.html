Frequently asked questions

Where are my records stored?
In the current version, your main records are stored on your device.

Can I import a backup?
The current version supports JSON exports but not imports or restoration.

Why are notifications not appearing?
Check ARC Daily notification permission in iOS Settings and your reminder options in the App.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the App ordinarily removes local records. Device backups and previously exported files need separate management.
